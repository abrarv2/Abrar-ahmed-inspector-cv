Inspection practice

Materials & fabrication

Inspect incoming materials, check mill test certificates and maintain traceability. Verify fit-up, weld consumables, fittings, valves and supports against drawings and specifications.

Welding & examination

Inspect shop and field welding, conduct welder qualification tests and coordinate third-party examinations. Monitor welder performance and follow up on repairs.

Testing & handover

Walk down lines against isometrics and P&IDs, classify A/B/C punch items, verify test limits and gauges, witness hydrotests and compile handover records.

Quality documentation

Prepare inspection and test plans, inspection requests and test packages. Review contractor procedures, report nonconformances and follow corrective actions through closeout.
